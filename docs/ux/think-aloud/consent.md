# Consent note

Read this to the participant (or let them read it), answer any questions,
and start only when they agree. Keep the signed or ticked copy with their
recording sheet.

---

**Testing the digital orrery**

Thank you for helping. This is a personal project: a solar-system simulator
that runs in a web browser. I am testing the **app, not you**. If something
is hard to do, that is a problem with the app and exactly what I want to
find.

**What you will do.** About 45 minutes. I will give you eight short tasks
to try, one at a time, and ask you to say out loud what you are thinking as
you go. After each task I will ask you to rate how easy it was, and at the
end you will answer ten short questions about the app as a whole.

**What I will record.** I will take notes. With your permission I will also
record the screen and your voice. Recordings stay on my own computer, are
used only to improve the app, and are deleted once the notes are written up
(within a month). Notes and any quotes I keep are anonymous: you are a
participant number, not a name.

**Your choices.** You can skip any task, take a break, or stop at any time
without giving a reason. If you stop, I will delete anything recorded on
request.
